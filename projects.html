    Candid — your ballot in simpler terms

    IrvineHacks · February 2026

    Ballot questions can be hard to understand because they are often long and wordy and don't explain the outcomes. Canddi asks five questions about your life and then explains each ballot measure in plain, simple langauge, personalized to you.

    • Personal explanations based on income, housing, location, and family.
    • Calculates household cost estimates
    • Every claim made links to exact legislative clause with a confidence score
    • Interactive 3D map of how policies affect your neighborhood
    • See how your politicians approach state and federal budgets
